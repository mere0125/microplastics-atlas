A note before leaving the water

Blank water
is not clean water.

NOAA calls this a presence-only archive. An empty region or year may simply mean that nobody contributed an observation there. The gaps describe the limits of our attention, not the absence of plastic.

The imbalance is visible inside the collection itself: 58.4% of records come from ocean water, 36.5% from Nurdle Patrol surveys, and 94.1% were recorded north of the equator.
